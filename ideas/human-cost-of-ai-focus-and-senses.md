# The human cost of AI, focus burnout and sensory overload

## Current understanding
Two predictions about what AI will do to human attention, one on the
producing side and one on the consuming side. Both are projections, in the
person's usual mode.

1. **Focus burnout.** Producing things with an agent drains focus, and it is
   very hard to stop: there is always something more that can be done, and
   the work is constant context switching. The person expects a new kind of
   burnout from it, a "focus burnout".
2. **Sensory overload.** AI can generate perfect media: songs and lyrics that
   trigger a neural response, videos, short clips, cartoons. Commercials
   will become hyper-optimized to stimulate the human brain, and living in
   that will be exhausting.

Common thread (agent's reading, not yet confirmed): in both cases the limit
is not the machine but the human attention on the other side of it, as the
cost of producing and of stimulating falls toward zero.
