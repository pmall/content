# Less and less between data and the result

## Current understanding
The distance between data and the result it can produce keeps shrinking.
What remains for the person is two jobs: present the data in a way an agent
can work on it, and formulate the desired result. Presenting the data the
right way is what lets diamond-style exploration (see the compute idea)
produce the result: it's the enabler, not a step inside the pattern.
Their example: the interaction graph they're building, supported by
publications (only an example, not the subject). This replaces an earlier
"value is data, not templates" framing the person rejected as not making
sense.
