# AI is blurry because intelligence is, "super agency" is a better name

## Current understanding
Everybody talks about AI and nobody means the same thing, and it is very
hard to settle on what it is. The person finds this obvious: intelligence
itself is already hard to define and nobody has the same definition. AI is
the artificial version of something we don't know exactly what it is, so it
is no wonder AI is blurry.

Their own move, after working a lot with AI: they would rather call it
**"super agency"**. Their definition of agency: **the ability to use
available tools efficiently to reach a goal.** (Not knowing or chatting:
acting with what is at hand.)

This ties directly to `agent-gets-an-environment`: an environment (a
filesystem and tools) is what agency needs to act on. Their definition
makes that link explicit; the connection is theirs, I only point at it.

It came as a realization while working on a slide deck about demystifying
AI (see `context/demystifying-ai-deck.md`), not as an explanation problem
they solved.

Applied (2026-10-23): asked by students whether super AI comes next year,
their answer is that it depends on what one calls super AI. Same move: the
term is undefined, so the timing question has no answer as asked.
