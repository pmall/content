# The phygitalisation of AI

## Current understanding
The person thinks AI is entering a "phygitalisation" phase: personal
assistants are arriving, and dedicated devices will follow. Their bet is that
value is made in the device, as Apple showed, so there may soon be "iPhones
of personal assistants". Trigger: news from the last week (see Research).
Their argument for why the device (2026-09-30): AI companies are not making money
for now, which is why many call AI a bubble (their view of common knowledge;
the cause they give is losing money on inference and subscriptions), and Apple is among the most
valuable companies in the world by making hardware. A way for an AI company
to profit is an AI-enabled device plus an AI subscription, like a phone plus
a phone subscription, but for AI. Counterpoint they raise themselves: an AI
assistant can already live in the phone, so these devices will have to be
original and suited to assistant use. They are not sure about this last part.
Same side of their thinking as the belief that assistants are not only for
work (see the shared-memory idea).

## Open threads

## Research (external, not personal perspective)
Checked 2026-09-30 by web search. Nothing here is the person's view.
- Meta: unveiled the Muse Charm at Connect (announced Sept 23-24, 2026), a palm-sized, Tamagotchi-style device for its Muse assistant, with a ~2-inch display, a fingerprint sensor to start talking, and a 5G modem. Reported available by December. Muse launched on phones and the web a few weeks earlier. Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/meta-debuts-a-dedicated-palm-sized-muse-charm-device-to-use-ai-on-the-go), [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/09/24/theres-nothing-like-it-meta-unveils-dedicated-charm-device-for-using-muse-ai/). The person said the device is for recording. The sources I saw describe talking to the assistant, not recording, so that detail is unconfirmed.
- OpenAI Dots (Sept 29, 2026, DevDay): "always-on" personal agents in ChatGPT, Slack and Teams, for Pro and some business tiers. Dots is software, and is described as independent of any specific hardware, so it is not a device. Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/29/openai-dots-agent/).
- OpenAI hardware, shape: reported (Bloomberg, Fast Company, Aug 2026) to be a display-less, donut-shaped, hockey-puck-sized smart speaker, about $300-400, designed by Jony Ive's LoveFrom, with moving parts. Per those reports it has not shipped: OpenAI said in a sworn statement it does not expect to ship before February 2027, and Apple sued over a metal-finishing technique. The person says OpenAI "already shipped a donut like device" that got little buzz. That does not match these sources, so unresolved. Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/what-is-openai-s-device-a-doughnut-shaped-speaker-that-costs-over-300), [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91587001/openai-hardware-donut-shaped-smart-speaker).
- OpenAI hardware: the Jony Ive team (from OpenAI's io acquisition) is building a screenless, pocket-sized device. Reports say it won't ship before the end of February 2027. Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/02/10/openais-jony-ive-designed-device-delayed-to-2027/), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/02/10/jony-ives-ai-hardware-is-delayed-to-2027-and-wont-be-called-io/).
